- LXX lemmas and parsing: Seth Kushniryk, lxx-morph (https://github.com/OpenScriptorium/lxx-morph), **CC BY 4.0**;
- analyses of the remaining forms by Perseus Morpheus (https://github.com/perseids-tools/morpheus), **CC BY-SA 3.0 US**;
- short meanings from Liddell–Scott–Jones in the Perseus Digital Library (https://github.com/PerseusDL/lexica), **CC BY-SA**;
- a manually checked list of forms (this project).
